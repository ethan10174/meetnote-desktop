## Session logging (REQUIRED — do this automatically at end of every session)
At the end of every session, WITHOUT being asked:
1. Append a summary to ~/MeetNote-Vault/MeetNote/sessions/YYYY-MM-DD.md (today's date)
   Include: what was changed, what was fixed, what was shipped, what needs testing
2. Update ~/MeetNote-Vault/MeetNote/bugs.md — move any fixed bugs to the Fixed section
3. Update ~/MeetNote-Vault/MeetNote/pending.md — check off completed tasks, add any new ones discovered

Do this at the end of the session before closing.
